# Product principles

1. **Threat-led, not control-led.** Start from who would attack this and how they would succeed. Controls appear in Keep Operating, not as the opening question.
2. **One spine.** STRIKE is the conversation. Common methodologies are imported, mapped, and exported — never forked into parallel wizards.
3. **Minutes, not workshops.** A complete assessment should be possible in under 20 minutes and still produce crown jewels, top threats, paths, ranked risk, resilience, and an action plan.
4. **Defensible artefacts.** Every generated item traces to Scope/Threat/Route/Impact/Keep Operating, an imported model, or an explicit inferred baseline.
5. **Speak GRC.** Centurion threat modelling (CISO Assistant EBIOS RM: assets, feared events, RO/TO, strategic/operational scenarios, risk scenarios) is a first-class export, not a screenshot.
6. **Offline by default.** The engine does not require an LLM. Optional AI can arrive later as a reviewer, not as the source of truth.
